## 🎮 About the Game

Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy is a third-person action-adventure game developed by Asobo Studio and published by Focus Entertainment. Released on August 27, 2026, it follows Sophia, a young plunderer exploring the Minotaur's Island in a story set 15 years before A Plague Tale: Requiem.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy**. This trainer provides optional gameplay assistance for Sophia's melee encounters, stealth sequences, exploration, and light-based environmental puzzles. It is designed for the single-player PC version and lets players reduce repetition while preserving the game's core combat, mystery, and exploration systems.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed story experience, easier melee encounters, faster exploration, or additional support while solving the island's light-based puzzles.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Health** — Prevents Sophia from losing health during combat, enemy encounters, and hazardous trials.
- **Stealth Protection** — Reduces or prevents detection during stealth sections and encounters where avoiding enemies is essential.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Unlimited Consumables** — Stops supported consumable resources from decreasing when used.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Perfect Parries** — Makes defensive parries more forgiving during Sophia's close-range fights.
- **Unlimited Combat Advantage** — Provides sustained support during dagger and sword encounters without changing the game's melee controls.
- **One-Hit Enemy Defeat** — Allows supported enemies to be defeated with a single successful attack.
- **Enemy Freeze** — Temporarily immobilizes supported enemies, giving Sophia time to reposition or continue an encounter safely.

### 🌍 World

- **Puzzle Assistance** — Supports progression through environmental puzzles involving the manipulation of light with the stolen Minoan sphere.
- **Safe Exploration** — Helps reduce interruptions from hostile encounters while exploring the island, its hidden paths, and ancient trials.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the trainer after loading into the campaign and reaching controllable gameplay. Movement assistance is intended for safer traversal of the island and its dangerous trails, while normal movement, climbing, hiding, and navigation controls remain unchanged.

### Tools & Inventory

Activate inventory-related options only after opening the relevant in-game resource or equipment interface. Unlimited consumables prevents supported resources from being spent, but it does not automatically grant story items or compatibility progression requirements.

### World & Environment

Exploration options are useful when moving through the Minotaur's Island, investigating secrets, and facing trials across Sophia's medieval journey and the ancient Minoan sequences. Puzzle assistance applies to challenges that use light manipulation and the Minoan sphere.

### Combat & Weapons

Enable combat options after an encounter begins. Unlimited health protects Sophia, Perfect Parries makes defensive timing more forgiving, and One-Hit Enemy Defeat accelerates supported fights. Enemy Freeze can be used to create space before continuing with dagger, sword, or grappling-hook tactics.
